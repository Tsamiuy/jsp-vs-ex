# Changelog

What changed in each version, newest first. Each version has two parts: **For players** is the release notes in plain words, and **Technical changes** is the full record. Versions are `X.Y.Z` (`installer/VERSION`); 0.1.0 to 0.6.1 are pre-releases, and the first full release will be 1.0.0. The technical part follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/). How to write an entry, with a template: `CONTRIBUTING.md`.

## [Unreleased]

### For players

**New**

- **Two-page Main Menu.** The Main Menu now shows five rows: Battle Modes, J-Customize, J-Point Shop, Gallery and Options. Battle Modes opens a second page with the game's battle rows (J-Adventure, Victory Road, Arcade, Free Battle, Online Battle) and two new rows: Training Mode, which starts a Free Battle with Training on, and Impossible Mode, which starts the Impossible 2 vs 1 fight. The row names are drawn by the port in the look of the game's own labels, and the help line and the button bar change with the page. It is on for everyone; the technical notes say how to switch it off and get the game's own menu back. Tried in scripted runs on one Windows PC with a keyboard; not tried online or with a second pad.
- **3v3 in Free Battle.** In Free Battle, a window on the 1 Player / 2 Players list asks for 2 vs 2 or 3 vs 3. A 3 vs 3 gives each team a third fighter on the Character Select (with a layout of its own), the Stage Select and the VS screen show three fighters per team, and the fight has six fighters. It works with 2 Players (Player 2 joins with Start). The other modes stay 2 vs 2. It is on for everyone together with the two-page Main Menu; the technical notes say how to switch it off. Tried in scripted runs on one Windows PC; "Fight again" and "Re-select" after a 3 vs 3 match and online play are not tried.
- **A box in the settings window for the new Main Menu.** On the Display tab, "Two-page Main Menu and 3v3" is ticked. Unticking it gives the game's own Main Menu back (and no 3v3). The window itself has not been seen on Windows yet.
- **Online Battle with rollback (experimental).** A new install is offline until you choose an online server: settings window, Online tab, re-psn, then a server you type or find with Find on LAN (this project names none). Then start the game, go to Main Menu > Battle Modes > Online Battle > Friendly Match (2 vs 2), one player creates a room and the other searches for it, and the fight runs on rollback netcode. The match goes through the server's relay when a direct link fails, and the tab suggests a random online name such as Player1234. Online settings you already have are not changed. A test build offers its own server: in the Online tab, one click on "Use <server name> (TEST BUILD)" turns it on, with rollback; nothing changes before that click. The Online tab says what the next start does and has a box to turn rollback off (the game's own netcode) or online off. Rollback needs the US disc; with the EU disc Online Battle uses the game's own netcode. A guide for testers is docs/TESTING_ONLINE.md (shipped as ONLINE-GUIDE.md). Tried with two copies on one Windows 11 PC; not tried between two PCs.
- **Ping under the timer in online fights.** During an online rollback fight, the round trip to the other player shows in ms under the fight timer, bottom right: green under 100 ms, yellow under 180, red above. It can be hidden in the settings file. Tried with two copies on one PC.
- **The two players are always on opposite teams online.** In Friendly Match the room could put both players on one team against two COM fighters; the second player now swaps team with a COM before the fight, on both PCs alike. Tried with two copies on one PC.
- **Discord shows what you are playing.** With the Discord app running, your Discord profile shows the screen you are on and, in a fight, both teams, red first, for example "Luffy & Naruto (+Rukia) vs Goku & Ichigo (+Neuro)", with the mode under it (Free Battle, Training, J-Adventure or "Online Friendly Match vs <name> (rollback)"), the KO score when the port counts KOs, and a timer that starts with each fight. While you host a Friendly Match room with space, friends can press Join or Ask to Join on your profile, or you can invite them from a Discord chat; the game then takes them to your room. It is on by default and talks only to the Discord app on your PC; docs/DISCORD.md says how to turn it off. Checked with host tests; Join and Invite between two PCs are not tried.
- **A short performance summary after each online match.** At the end of every rollback match the game log gets five `[perf]` lines: whether the match went direct or through the server, how long a button press took to show and to reach the other PC, how many rollbacks there were and how deep, the pauses over 50 ms, and how long a rollback took to show. The support bundle carries them, so testers on two PCs can send numbers instead of impressions. They only measure; nothing about the match changes. Not yet collected between two PCs.

**Changed**

- **2 Players split screen runs faster.** A 2 Players 2 vs 2 fight on a busy stage ran at about 18 to 24 frames per second on one Windows PC and now runs at about 28 to 30 with the default settings; 1 Player is the same as before. It uses 16 MB more graphics upload memory. Measured on one PC only.
- **Clearer way into online play.** Setup's last page and the end of the first build now say where online play is: Main Menu > Battle Modes > Online Battle > Friendly Match (2 vs 2), where one player picks Create a Room and the other Search for a Room. The Online tab of the settings window and the messages at the top of the main menu use plainer words (for example, with an EU disc they now say that rollback needs the US disc). The tester guide, docs/TESTING_ONLINE.md (shipped as ONLINE-GUIDE.md), starts with five steps and a list of what to do when something goes wrong.

**Fixed**

- **Port overlay pictures no longer flicker back.** A picture the port draws over the game and changes every frame (an animation) could show an older picture for a frame or two instead of the new one. It now waits for the new picture. Seen in test runs on one Windows PC only.
- **KO sound no longer repeats with 4 or 5 KOs to win.** In a Free Battle with "KOs to win" set to 4 or 5, the sound of the KO bar (the "one KO left" sound) could play over and over for the rest of the match. It now plays once for each KO. Tried in test fights on one Windows PC; not tried online.
- **No more "Communicating..." forever after the other player quit mid-fight.** After "Connection has timed out." and OK, the next Online Battle could stay on "Communicating..." until the game was restarted, because the game waited for its old connection to close and it never did. It now closes at once, so Online Battle works again without a restart. Tested with the toolkit's network tests; not yet tried in a game.
- **Black screen after an online match (often a rematch) fixed.** Rarely, one PC stayed on a black screen after a match and left the room about 30 seconds later, because it was one button sample short of finishing. Only after a match that ended normally, the game now gives that PC an empty sample (no buttons pressed), so it moves on within about a second; this cannot change a result. Tested with host tests; not yet tried in a game.
- **No more pauses for both players when one PC meets new graphics in an online fight.** New graphics are now prepared in the background in rollback fights too, so a PC that has not seen a character or effect yet no longer holds up both players; an effect may be missing for a moment the first time it appears. Measured with two copies on one PC, one of them with an empty graphics cache, on a simulated link, three runs each way: pauses at the start of a game tick 51 -> 0, seconds under 29.5 frames per second 32 -> 6-9, rollbacks 42 -> 1-10. Not yet measured between two PCs. The offline warm-up fight is no longer needed.
- **Online fight sound is more robust.** In a rollback fight the game could move on to the next tick just before it had taken the last sound block of the tick before, and after a change of sound device in a fight (for example headphones plugged in) the sound could skip ahead. Both are fixed. Tested with a stress test of the sound code on one Windows PC; not yet heard in a game.
- **The automatic input delay counts each ping once.** It used to count the last ping answer again every time it looked, also when no new answer had come, so after a short gap in the connection it could keep following an old reading. Tested with host tests; not yet compared between two PCs.
- **A frozen online match ends on both PCs.** A match whose game froze for good could leave the other player on "Reconnecting..." forever. Now the match ends on both PCs about 60 seconds after the picture stopped: the other player sees "Opponent disconnected" (or the reason), and on the frozen PC the match usually ends too, about 5 seconds later. Tested with host tests; not yet tried in a game.
- **A connection drop at a KO no longer counts as a finished match unless the KO was already confirmed.** When the other PC dropped while this PC had only guessed a KO or time-up (mostly when the other player was not pressing anything), the match ended as "Match over". It now ends as "Opponent disconnected"; a KO or time-up still ends as "Match over" only if this PC had already confirmed it with both players' real inputs before the disconnect. Tested with host tests; not yet tried in a game.
- **Free Battle keeps your picks after an online match.** An online match that ended by a desync or a disconnect, or opening the lobby, cleared the last offline Free Battle character picks, and an online match right after an offline Free Battle could end at once with "Desync detected". The picks now stay, and online matches no longer compare them. Tested with host tests; not yet tried in a game.
