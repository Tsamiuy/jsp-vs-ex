### What Second Gear changes (inferred)

Rapid Fire uses rows 28 and 29 (20 per hit) before Jet Gatling's 30 and 31 outside Second Gear. Inside it, the same taps go straight to rows 30 and 31. The finisher moves from row 27 (400) to row 75 (500). R1 + Circle uses row 32 outside and 33 or 34 inside. The change showed up between 49 and 67 seconds after the power-up, so it may last about that long.

### Damage that differs from the table

- 176 hits did exactly half the table's damage. The hit record already holds the halved number at +0x64 and +0x68, so it's decided before the damage is applied.
- Not explained by opponent's health, Luffy's Stamina or time since Second Gear. Position in a combo doesn't explain it either. The ultimate shows it most clearly: every 100 after its first hit becomes 50.
- There is no double damage. Hits that looked doubled are two hit records applied in the same frame, and the two numbers add up exactly (8 of 877 hits; checked with a local script).

R1 + Circle's rows overlap across modes in this table. The maintainer explains why: pressing Circle many times during R1 + Circle (not holding) moves the move into its Second Gear version, and the same happens again from Second Gear. So rows 32, 33, 34 and 36 probably step up with the mode and those presses, not with holding as first read. Which presses lead to which row isn't confirmed.

**Second Gear refills Stamina:** R1 + Cross set it to 1000 in 9 of 10 uses (from as low as 375). The tenth use started at 977, too close to full to show a jump.

**Just Guard:** 19 uses, seen as motions started with action 21 (`mot` lines), not as `act` lines. The first raised Stamina from 674 to 828, which fits the table's -150 plus normal recovery. Stamina was at or near full for most of the others, so their refund can't be seen. The maintainer reports that Luffy teleports on a Just Guard.

**Dash Gatling with stick down:** Circle during a dash stayed on row 66 and led to row 27 slot 2, with R2 and L3 held in some tries. No new row appeared for Double Barrel.

**Rows still not seen in either session:** 15, 17, 25, 35, 46 to 60, 62, 65, 67, 70, 71.
